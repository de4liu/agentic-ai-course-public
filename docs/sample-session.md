# Sample session: How AI Works and How to Work With It

Week 1, Session 1. The first session of the course, taught on October 28,
2026. This is what a concept session looks like: short lecture, live demos,
and discussion.

## What this session covers

- How a large language model actually produces an answer, and why fluent
  output is not the same as a correct one.
- Variation, grounding and hallucination, demonstrated live, not just
  described.
- A working vocabulary for talking about AI systems precisely: what they
  are good at, where they fail, and why.
- How to work *with* the model: prompting as business communication, not
  programming.

## A typical week in this course

| | Session 1 (concepts) | Session 2 (hands-on) |
|---|---|---|
| **Format** | Short lecture, live demos, case discussion | Guided build with no-code tools |
| **You leave with** | Vocabulary and mental models | A working artifact or design |

Every other week also includes a short reflection post. Three weeks include
a build sprint; Week 7 is the AI Innovation Showcase.
